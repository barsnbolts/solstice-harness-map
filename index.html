Engineering reference

The Link connector panel follows the manufacturer’s Quick Start Guide, page 5: A, B, E, then communications. Cavity numbering looks into the ECU headers; the mating side of a detached loom plug is mirrored. Exterior contours and spacing are schematic.

The OEM faces follow GM document 1837903’s archived drawings. Exact vehicle year, installed ECU label, harness modifications and actual viewing orientation remain unverified. Matched male header keying and sourcing remain HOLD.

Dashed highlights are reserved or unresolved. A cavity without a drawn route is not automatically interchangeable spare I/O. New port injection has separate wiring; stock DI and HPFP power leads remain isolated. Future DI needs a compatible external driver and characterization. Accessory interfaces, power distribution, ABS/brake assist/CAN dependencies still require verification.
